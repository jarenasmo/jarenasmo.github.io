About

I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of Southern California. I hold a Bachelor's degree and a Master's degree in Economics from the University of Chile. Previously, I was an Instructor Professor in the Department of Economics at the University of Chile, and an Economic Advisor to the Board and the Vice Presidency of the Central Bank of Chile.

You can contact me at jiarenas@usc.edu .
